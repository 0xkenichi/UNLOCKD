## What this does not model

- Unlock schedules of the whole token (the drift term): not yet in the data, so v2 is run with zero unlock drift.
- Intraday moves: daily closes stand in for the oracle price, so a gap inside one day is seen at the close.
- Partner buyback, insurance and tranches: losses are reported before any of them.
- Real borrower behaviour: borrowers here are adversarial (always max out, never cure).
